Three silent killers are quietly accumulating inside your code base, change amplification, cognitive load, and the paralyzing threat of unknown unknowns. And all this friction traces back to a specific human instinct.
The urge to operate as a tactical tornado, prioritizing the immediate deadline over the long-term integrity of the design. We hit our sprint goals and celebrate the velocity, while leaving behind a massive wake of brittle workarounds and structural rot that the next developer will have to navigate. The short-term speed gained by these tactical shortcuts inevitably creates permanent friction, tengling the code base until adding the smallest feature grinds the entire engineering team to a halt.
Now unleash modern automated tooling into that fragile environment, and you pour gasoline directly onto a silent architectural crisis. Artificial intelligence excels at generating output that mimics the aesthetics of clean code, producing tiny functions and descriptive names, but it consistently fails to achieve architectural depth.
