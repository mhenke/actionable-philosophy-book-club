# Welcome to the Actionable Philosophy Book Club

This site is your hub for every book-club session. Here's how it works.

## What you'll find on the Dashboard

- **Next Meeting card** — Shows the upcoming session: watch the video primer, then review the slides which set the stage and open the discussion. After the session, find related podcasts and resources.
- **The Archive** — Past sessions with all their materials. Click "Meeting Notes" to read the session notes and materials.
- **Knowledge Base** — Reference docs: glossary of terms, design principles, and AI prompt templates.
- **The Horizon** — Planned sessions that aren't yet scheduled.

## Meeting structure

Each session follows the same flow:

1. **Watch the video primer** — A short recap of the assigned reading.
2. **Review the slides** — Sets the stage, then opens the floor for group discussion.
3. **Go deeper with podcasts** — After the session, explore audio discussions:
   - **Deep Dive** — Solo exploration of a session topic (~15-20 min)
   - **Debate** — Structured dialogue between two perspectives (~20-30 min)
   - **Critique** — Critical analysis of arguments and trade-offs (~15-20 min)

## Quick tips

- All media links are checked for availability — if a file hasn't been uploaded yet, you'll see a friendly notice.
- Video files play inline — close the player or press Esc when done. Your progress is saved automatically.
- Share a direct link to any media asset by clicking it — the URL updates for easy sharing.
- Use the copy-link button in the reader header to share specific session notes.
- Press Esc from the reader view to return to the dashboard.
- **Onboarding banner** — The onboarding banner appears on first visit. Restore it from the Dashboard using the 'Restore onboarding banner' button in the Knowledge Base (same card style as Glossary).
