# AI Workflow Ideas

Potential ways to use AI to enhance the Actionable Philosophy Book Club experience.

## 🧪 Current Workflows

### Chapter Summarization
- Use AI to generate high-level summaries of readings to save time for busy participants.
- *Template:* `templates/prompts/slide-deck-prompt-template.md`

### Discussion Stimulation
- Generate thought-provoking "Essential Questions" based on the text to drive deep conversations.
- *Template:* `templates/prompts/five-essential-questions-prompt.md`

### Action Item Extraction
- Automatically identify practical tasks and follow-ups from raw meeting transcripts or notes.

## 🚀 Future Ideas

- **Cross-Chapter Analysis:** Use AI to find contradictions or reinforcements between different chapters.
- **Code Audit:** Use AI to scan our own projects for the "Red Flags" mentioned in the book (e.g., Information Leakage, Shallow Modules).
- **Interactive Q&A:** Set up a local RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) system for the book club materials.
